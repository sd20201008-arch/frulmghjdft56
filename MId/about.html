Who is Benito Antonio Martínez Ocasio?

The Global King of Reggaeton

Born in Almirante Sur, Vega Baja, Puerto Rico, Bad Bunny transformed from a grocery store bagger posting songs on SoundCloud into a global cultural phenomenon.

Known for his conversational, deep-toned delivery and unapologetic fashion, Benito broken records worldwide without altering his native language or sound, bringing Latin urban music to the highest global standard.

Key Career Milestones

Grammy Winner

Multiple Grammy Awards recipient and the first artist to earn an Album of the Year nomination for a Spanish-language record (*Un Verano Sin Ti*).

#1 Most Streamed

Crowned Spotify's most-streamed artist globally for three consecutive years (2020, 2021, and 2022).

Stadium History

Broke all-time records for highest-grossing tour in a single calendar year during his *World's Hottest Tour*.
